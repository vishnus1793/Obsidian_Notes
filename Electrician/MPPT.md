maximum Power point Tracking is full form of the word MPPT 
It is an intelligent DC to DC regulator that optimizes power transfer from a DC source 

Looked for This for charging the battery from the solar Panel


What is MPPT?

- Solar panels do not produce a fixed amount of power.

- Power output changes based on the voltage and current load.

- There is a specific "sweet spot" on a panel's power curve where voltage multiplied by current reaches its absolute peak.

- MPPT uses an algorithm and a DC-to-DC converter to continuously hunt for and lock onto this moving target. 

List of the Algorithms:
- **Perturb and Observe (P&O)**
- **Incremental Conductance (INC)**
- **Fractional Open-Circuit Voltage (\(V_{oc}\))**
- **Fractional Short-Circuit Current (\(I_{sc}\))**
- **Fuzzy Logic Control (FLC)**
- **Artificial Neural Networks (ANN)**
- **Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO)**
- **Genetic Algorithms (GA)**
- **Hill Climbing**
- **Ripple Correlation Control (RCC)**
- **Sliding Mode Control (SMC)**
