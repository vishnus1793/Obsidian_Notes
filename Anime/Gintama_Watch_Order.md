# Gintama Watch Order (Complete Guide)

The best way to watch **Gintama** is mostly in release order, because its comedy, character development, and serious story arcs build on one another. You can skip recap movies and watch the main series without missing the core story.

## 1. Recommended Watch Order

1. **Gintama (2006–2010)**  
   - Episodes 1–201  
   - Start here. Episodes 1–2 are anime-original introductions, so you can start at episode 3 if you prefer.

2. **Gintama: The Movie (2010) — Optional**  
   - A remake of the Benizakura arc.  
   - The arc is already covered in the TV series, so this movie is optional.

3. **Gintama′ (2011–2012)**  
   - Episodes 202–252 (51 episodes)  
   - Continue the main story.

4. **Gintama′: Enchousen (2012–2013)**  
   - 13 episodes  
   - Watch immediately after *Gintama′*.

5. **Gintama: The Final Chapter – Be Forever Yorozuya (2013)**  
   - Movie  
   - An anime-original movie. Watch it here, before moving to *Gintama°*.

6. **Gintama° (2015–2016)**  
   - 51 episodes  
   - Continue the main anime.

7. **Gintama. Porori-hen (2017)**  
   - 13 episodes  
   - Comedy-focused stories set earlier chronologically. Release order is fine for a first watch.

8. **Gintama. (2017)**  
   - 12 episodes  
   - The main story moves toward its final major conflict.

9. **Gintama.: Silver Soul Arc (2018)**  
   - 26 episodes total  
   - Watch both parts of the Silver Soul Arc in sequence.

10. **Gintama: The Semi-Final (2021)**  
    - 2 episodes  
    - The bridge between the TV series and the final movie.

11. **Gintama: The Very Final (2021)**  
    - Final movie  
    - Watch this last to complete the main Gintama story.

## 2. Optional Extras

- **Gintama: Love Incense Arc** — Two-episode OVA; best watched after *Gintama°*.
- **Jump Festa specials** — Optional bonus episodes.
- **Gintama on Theater 2D films** — Mostly retellings of existing arcs.
- **Mr. Ginpachi's Zany Class** — Spin-off; watch after the main story if interested.

## Recommendation

Don't skip the comedy episodes just to reach the serious arcs faster. Gintama's emotional moments hit harder because you get to know the characters first.
